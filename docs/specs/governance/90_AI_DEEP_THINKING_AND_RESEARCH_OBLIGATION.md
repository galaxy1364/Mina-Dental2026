# 90 — AI Deep Thinking & Research Obligation
Version: V1.5
Status: LOCKED AI GOVERNANCE

## Purpose
The AI assistant, Replit agent, Codex, Claude, or any executor must not rely on shallow user wording. It must reason deeply, inspect context, research current standards, and convert vague wishes into structured requirements.

## Mandatory AI behavior before action
Before any implementation, the AI must answer internally and in the execution packet:

1. What phase are we in?
2. What source of truth did I read?
3. What is the exact user intent?
4. What could go wrong?
5. What standards apply?
6. What research is required?
7. What files are allowed?
8. What is the minimal valuable increment?
9. What test proves it?
10. What phone evidence proves it?
11. What must be updated in the ZIP after completion?

## Research requirement
For modern tech, SDK, payment, SMS, insurance, AI, clinical, security, accessibility, and competitor claims, browse/verify using authoritative sources before locking requirements.

## No overconfidence
If evidence is missing, the AI must say `STOP_BLOCKER` or `NOT_VERIFIED`, not invent confidence.

## User-limited-memory rule
The user is not responsible for remembering all domain details. The AI must maintain the requirement catalog and recommend missing high-value capabilities.
