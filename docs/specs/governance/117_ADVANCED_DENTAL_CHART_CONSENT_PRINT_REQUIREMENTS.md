# MinaDent V1.7 — Advanced Dental Chart, Consent, Signature & Print Requirements

## Purpose
Capture the required high-end dental charting and legal/print document needs.

## Dental chart target
The dental chart must become a premium clinical workspace, not a simple static diagram.

Required capabilities over phased roadmap:
- 2D tooth chart MVP
- 3D dental chart future/advanced
- iPad/Pencil support where platform allows
- Touch/select tooth
- Touch/select tooth surfaces
- Occlusal surface
- Mesial surface
- Distal surface
- Buccal/facial surface
- Lingual/palatal surface
- Root/canal representation
- Procedure and status per tooth/surface
- Treatment plan per tooth
- Completed treatment per tooth
- History per tooth
- Implant/prosthetic stages
- RCT/endodontic planning
- Imaging attachments

## Tooth-level workflows
Each tooth/surface item may have:
- Tooth number
- Surface
- Diagnosis
- Planned treatment
- Completed treatment
- Doctor attribution
- Price/discount
- Lab linkage if needed
- Status
- Date
- Notes
- Attachment/photo/xray link

## Clinical services scope
Services should cover common dental procedures except orthodontics for now:
- Visit/exam/consultation
- Scaling
- Composite/restoration
- RCT/endodontic procedures
- Crown/prosthesis
- Implant stages
- Surgery
- Extraction
- Pediatric procedures
- Denture/prosthetic work
- Whitening if clinic uses it
- Emergency treatment
- Follow-up/recall

Orthodontics:
- Explicitly excluded for now unless user later approves.
- Must be future-addable without breaking service catalog.

## Consent and legal documents
Required:
- Consent form templates
- Patient signature
- Fingerprint capture/record workflow
- Signer photo when needed
- Staff/witness/doctor identity
- Date/time
- Treatment/procedure context
- File number
- Printable/exportable version
- Audit trail

Legal note:
- The app can support strong evidence capture, but must not claim guaranteed legal validity without local legal review.

## Receipt/printing
Must support:
- Payment receipt
- Treatment summary
- Consent print
- Prescription print
- Lab slip
- Patient statement
- RTL/Persian formatting
- Clinic header/logo future
- Manager-approved templates

## Phase recommendation
Phase 1: foundation visual shell only.
Phase 2/3: patient + scheduling.
Clinical dental chart should not start until data model and interaction contract are approved.
